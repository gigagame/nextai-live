Support

Contact 2NEXTAI.

Use the guidance below for digital-product access, payment-related questions or website feedback.

Purchase or download support

If you purchased a 2NEXTAI digital product through PayPal, use the seller/contact option associated with your PayPal transaction or receipt. Include the transaction ID, purchase date and a short description of the issue so the order can be identified.

Never send passwords, full card numbers or other sensitive payment credentials.

Common support issues

  • Payment completed but the download page did not open.
  • The purchased file cannot be opened or downloaded.
  • A duplicate payment appears on the transaction history.
  • A site link or resource appears broken.

For payment disputes or PayPal account matters, PayPal's own support and resolution tools may also be required.

General inquiries

2NEXTAI is currently a static website and does not collect messages through an on-site contact form. A dedicated public support channel may be added later. Until then, purchase-related support should use the transaction-linked contact route described above.
